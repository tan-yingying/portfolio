Changi Airport×Spider-Man: Far From Home

About the Project

Spider-Man: Far From Home was a film all about travel, making Changi Airport -- the best airport in the world -- a great fit for the marketing campaign. The result of the campaign was significant ATL and PR exposure for the film across Singapore.


The activation featured large-scale locale setups of Spider-Man locations, Spider-Man meet-and-greets, actual Spider-Man suits and props on display, a merchandise range working with Spider-Man licensees, and a Spider-Man: Far From Home VR game with PlayStation.

Program Highlights

Significant ATL and PR exposure
Large-scale Spider-Man location setups at Changi Airport
Spider-Man meet-and-greet experiences
Actual Spider-Man suits and props on display
Spider-Man merchandise range with licensees
Spider-Man: Far From Home VR game with PlayStation
