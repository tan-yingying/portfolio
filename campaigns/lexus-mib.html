Lexus×Men In Black: International

About the Project

Lexus partnered with Sony Pictures for Men In Black: International, featuring the Lexus RC F as the heroes' vehicle of choice in the film. The partnership extended to a co-branded marketing campaign across multiple APAC markets, positioning Lexus as the premium automotive brand aligned with the MIB franchise's themes of style, performance, and cutting-edge technology.

Program Highlights

In-film product placement of Lexus RC F
Dealer invite events and exclusive screening experiences
Co-branded digital and outdoor campaign across APAC
Regional partnership overview presentations across markets
